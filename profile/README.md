# SoundBoost Suite: AI Mastering Platform

Welcome to the SoundBoost AI (previously Diktatorial Suite).

SoundBoost is an AI-powered <a href="https://soundboost.com">AI mastering</a> platform that lets you control sound using natural language instead of complex knobs and plugins.

Built by musicians and engineers, it transforms raw tracks into release-ready masters in seconds.

---

## ✨ Prompt-to-Mastering

Describe your sound. That’s it.

Examples:

- "Breathing highs, wide stereo field"
- "Make it punchy and club-ready"
- "Bring vocals forward with warmth"

SoundBoost translates your words into real mastering decisions using advanced DSP and AI.  

---

## 🧠 How it works

1. Upload your track (.wav, .mp3, etc.)
2. Describe your desired sound with a prompt  
3. AI builds and applies a mastering chain  
4. Preview, revise, and finalize  

No DAW. No presets. Just sound and intent.
